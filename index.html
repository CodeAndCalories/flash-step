THE FLASH-STEP
[ THE DARKNESS ]

You are trapped in a maze.
You cannot see without your camera flash.
Find the glowing green door to escape.

[ THE FLASH ]

Press and hold FLASH to illuminate.
The longer you hold — the more it sees you.
Brighter flash = faster enemies.
Collect batteries to increase brightness.

[ THE THINGS IN THE DARK ]

THE STALKER follows your light.
THE MIMIC follows your footsteps.
THE BLIND ONE follows your sound.
They move when you illuminate. Don't look too long.

[ CONTROLS ]

PC: WASD / Arrows to move · Mouse to look
    SPACE to flash · ESC to pause
Mobile: D-pad to move · Turn buttons to look
        FLASH button to illuminate

[ TIPS ]

· Listen for the heartbeat — it means something is close
· Your first flash reveals a map. Use it wisely.
· Standing still in darkness makes you harder to find
· Short flashes are safer than long ones
